## What is CareerForge Analytics?

CareerForge Analytics is an immersive career acceleration programme combining
real-world job simulations, expert mentorship, live projects, performance
reviews, and career coaching to bridge the gap between learning and
employment.

## What tracks are available?

CareerForge offers a Training track and an Internship track. Internship
applicants additionally submit a CV, GitHub/portfolio URL, and LinkedIn URL.

## How long does the programme run?

The combined curriculum runs 12 weeks, covering Excel, SQL, Power BI, and
Python for Data Analytics, delivered on a Monday/Wednesday/Friday cadence.

## How do I apply?

Visit the Apply page on the CareerForge website, select Training or
Internship, and complete the application form.
